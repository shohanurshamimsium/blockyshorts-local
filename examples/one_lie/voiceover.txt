In this world, you get one lie. Whatever you say, everyone believes it forever. Everyone except you. Most people waste it.

My coworker Jake told our boss he'd already finished the report. He hadn't started. Now our boss quotes numbers from a report that doesn't exist.

My aunt told her landlord the rent was paid. That was eleven years ago. She still hasn't paid it.

My dad never used his. Everyone said he was wasting it. He wore the same coat for nine winters, worked two jobs, and every time I asked how he was, he said the same thing. I'm fine.

When I turned eighteen, I begged him. Dad, use your lie. Tell the world we're rich. He just laughed. Not yet.

Then Mom got sick. He sat by her bed every night. On the last night, he held her hand and whispered something I couldn't hear. She smiled. Then she fell asleep.

Years later, I found his old phone in a drawer. There was one voice note. I used my lie tonight. I told her I'd be okay. She believed it. [pause] Everyone believes me. Everyone except me.

He has said I'm fine every day for twenty years. And I believed him. Everyone did. Nobody checked on him once.

[speed_slow] I'm calling him right now. If you got one lie, who would you tell it to?
